# Why ROLL?

One day, I realized something that bothered me: my friend couldn't physically roll a die to participate in tabletop games.

Not because they didn't understand the game. Not because they couldn't make the decisions. Not because they didn't belong at the table.

**They just couldn't physically roll a little piece of plastic.**

I found that stupid. Technology has come a long way, yet something as simple as rolling a die can still be a physical barrier to participating in a game.

So I'm building something about it.

ROLL started as an attempt to remove that physical barrier. It has since grown into a broader idea: tabletop gaming should not require everyone to interact with the game in exactly the same physical way.

ROLL is therefore designed for everyone. If you need it for accessibility, it should give you independence. If you don't, it should still be something you genuinely want to use.

**Nobody should feel like they're merely being accommodated. Everyone should feel like they're playing.**
