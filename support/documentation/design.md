## Design Consideration
People with physical disabilities can face barriers to enjoying tabletop games, and sometimes that barrier can be as simple as rolling a dice. I find it frustrating for all the technological advancements we have gone through.
And so, I have taken it upon myself along with my mentors at [RISC-V Ottawa (RVO)](https://riscvottawa.ca/) to help me build a fully open sourced dice for all.

If you feel like contributing to this project, please familiarize yourself with the project and repository before getting started. Understanding the design decisions and documentation is important to making meaningful contributions.
